  Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

Qdrant Web UI (dashboard)

including its tokenizer
  https://huggingface.co/google/siglip2-base-patch16-256

swift-transformers (Tokenizers), and its dependencies swift-crypto,
swift-collections, swift-jinja and swift-huggingface
  https://github.com/huggingface/swift-transformers
  Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
  yyjson (https://github.com/ibireme/yyjson), MIT License.
